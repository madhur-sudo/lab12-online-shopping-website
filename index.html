Our story

Why we started

Morrow Supply started because finding decent desk gear in India — without wading through three hundred near-identical listings on a marketplace — was harder than it had any right to be. The imported options were priced like luxury items. The budget options died within months. The gap between "cheap and unreliable" and "expensive and imported" was real, and nobody seemed particularly interested in filling it.

So we do the sifting. We test the things that look promising, return the ones that disappoint, and stock the ones that earn their place. We're not trying to carry everything — we'd rather carry fewer things and stand behind all of them. If it's on the site, someone here uses it, or used it long enough to know it works.
