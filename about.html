About Morrow Supply

We sell things that work, backed by people who actually tested them.

"Finding decent desk gear in India without sifting through endless marketplace listings was harder than it should be."

Why we exist

Morrow Supply started out of frustration. Not the kind that makes for a tidy founding story — just the ordinary kind. We wanted good keyboards, decent speakers, and a laptop stand that didn't wobble. What we found instead was a lot of choices between cheap-and-breaks-fast and imported-and-expensive-for-no-clear-reason.

So we decided to do it differently. We test things before we sell them. We don't list a product because a supplier is offering a good margin — we list it because we've used it and would use it again. The range stays small on purpose. We'd rather carry thirty things confidently than three hundred things just to fill a catalogue.

Everything ships from Bengaluru. Orders above ₹2,500 ship free. If something arrives broken or wrong, we fix it — no forms, no waiting, no arguments.

What we carry

Six categories, chosen because they cover the things most people need when they're setting up or improving a desk.

  • Peripherals

    Mice and keyboards are the two things you touch most on a computer, so it's worth getting them right. We carry wired and wireless options across a price range that doesn't require a loan. Mechanical keyboards only where the switch quality is actually worth it.

  • Audio

    Headphones for focused work and speakers for the room. We test for call quality as much as music quality because most people use both. Nothing in our audio range is chosen for bass boost numbers; we care more about whether the thing is comfortable to wear for six hours.

  • Lighting

    Desk lamps that give you clean, adjustable light without flickering or producing an unpleasant colour temperature. Eye strain is real; a decent lamp is one of the cheaper ways to fix it. We've skipped anything that felt cheap in the hand or inconsistent in the light.

  • Laptop Accessories

    Stands, hubs, and sleeves. A laptop stand costs about ₹1,900 here and probably saves you a physio visit. The USB-C hubs we carry have all been tested for heat management, because a hub that gets too hot is a hub that breaks your work. Sleeves fit actual laptop sizes, not just what the manufacturer listed.

  • Desk Accessories

    Desk mats, cable organisers, and the small things that stop a desk from feeling chaotic. This is the category where we say the most honest thing: a lot of this gear exists elsewhere for less, but ours is sized properly, made of materials that last, and won't have you buying a replacement in four months.

  • Everyday Tech

    The practical connective tissue of a modern setup — USB-C hubs, cable management, charging solutions, and the stuff you only notice when it stops working. We try to carry items that are genuinely useful rather than technically impressive.
